Mod List

Every mod in the pack, grouped by what it does for you

COBBLEVERSE is built from roughly 137 mods — 136 of them active — running on Fabric for Minecraft 1.21.1. The heart of the pack is Cobblemon 1.7.3, which puts Pokémon in the world to find, battle, catch, and evolve. Around it sit the flagship additions that define the pack: Radical Cobblemon Trainers fills the world with over 1,500 battle-ready trainers drawn from Radical Red, Unbound, and Brilliant Diamond/Shining Pearl (about 380 of them are wired into the COBBLEVERSE story series — the rest belong to those optional side series); Mega Showdown brings Mega Evolution, Z-Moves, Terastallization, Dynamax, and Ultra Burst into battle; Legendary Monuments adds the structures and mechanics for earning Legendary and Mythical Pokémon; Cobblemon Raid Dens adds raid boss battles; CobbleDollars powers the shop economy; Cobbreeding adds breeding; and TM Craft makes TMs craftable.

The rest of the roster plays support: world generation and structures, furniture and decor for building your own gyms and Pokécenters, quality-of-life tweaks, interface and audio-visual polish, a thick layer of performance mods, and the libraries holding it all together. Every entry below is listed with its exact jar file name from the pack's mods folder.
