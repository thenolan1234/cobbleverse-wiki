Legendaries

How every legendary and mythical is actually obtained in COBBLEVERSE

Legendary and Mythical Pokémon in COBBLEVERSE are never simple random encounters. Most are tied to the Poké Radar system from LumyMon (lumymon:poke_radar, crafted from iron and redstone around a Locator Chip found in bastion treasure, end city and ominous trial vault loot): upgrade the radar with type gems and key materials to track a legendary's structure, then bring the right summoning item to the altar inside. Others are guarded by the Legendary Monuments shrine system, hidden behind boss trainers of Team Rocket and Team Galactic, or simply roam the wild as ultra-rare natural spawns. Every method below is taken from the pack's own spawn data, recipes, loot tables and advancements.

Articuno

Craft the Articuno Radar (Poké Radar + Ice Gem + Never-Melt Ice + Blue Ice) to locate Articuno's tower. The Glacier Feather is found inside the tower itself — use it at the altar to summon Articuno at level 50.

Key items: Articuno Radar Glacier Feather Poke Radar

Zapdos

Craft the Zapdos Radar (Poké Radar + Electric Gem + Cell Battery + gold) to locate Zapdos' tower. Retrieve the Thunder Feather inside and summon Zapdos (level 50–60) at the altar.

Key items: Zapdos Radar Thunder Feather

Moltres

Craft the Moltres Radar (Poké Radar + Fire Gem + Fire Stone + blaze powder) — its tower stands in the Nether. The Ember Feather found inside summons Moltres (level 50–60) at the altar.

Key items: Moltres Radar Ember Feather

Galarian Birds (Dyna Tree)

The Dyna Tree Radar (Poké Radar + Flying Gem + Dynamax Candy + glowstone) locates the Dyna Tree, where all three Galarian birds spawn at level 60: Galarian Articuno at night, Galarian Zapdos during thunderstorms, and Galarian Moltres by day.

Key items: Dyna Tree Radar

Mewtwo

Recreate Team Rocket's experiment: place Ancient DNA (dropped by Gym Leader Giovanni) and a Cloning Catalyst (dropped by Rocket Admin Atena) into Cobblemon's Resurrection Machine. The revived Mewtwo has 2 guaranteed perfect IVs; a Radiant Cloning Catalyst yields a Shiny Mewtwo with 4. The Rocket Key reveals the Team Rocket Tower's coordinates.

Key items: Ancient Dna Cloning Catalyst Cloning Catalyst Shiny Rocket Key

Mew

Craft the Origin Fossil from any Cobblemon fossil, an Ancient Origin Ball and Ancient DNA, then awaken Mew (level 75–90) with it at the Mew Shrine in the Origin Temple.

Key items: Origin Fossil Ancient Dna

Raikou, Entei &amp; Suicune

All three Legendary Beasts spawn naturally at the Burned Tower structure — each with an equal one-in-three chance, level 60, ultra-rare bucket. The Burned Tower Radar (Poké Radar + Fire Gem + flint and steel) locates it.

Key items: Burned Radar

Lugia

The Radio Card locates the Rocket Radio Tower in Johto, where Team Rocket boss Giovanni drops the Silver Wing. Use it at Whirl Island (found with the Lugia Radar) to summon Lugia at level 60–70.

Key items: Silver Wing Lugia Radar Radio Card

Ho-Oh

Defeat the Old Sage for the Rainbow Wing, then summon Ho-Oh (level 60–70) at the Bell Tower, located with the Ho-Oh Radar (Poké Radar + Flying Gem + all five stat feathers + wind charge).

Key items: Rainbow Wing Hooh Radar

Celebi

Defeat the legendary trainer Red to claim the GS Ball, which reveals Celebi (level 75–80) within the Ilex Shrine.

Key items: Gs Ball

Regirock, Regice &amp; Registeel

Each golem sleeps in its own temple, located with a dedicated radar built from the matching type gem plus tumblestones: Regirock Radar, Regice Radar and Registeel Radar. The golems await inside at level 40–50.

Key items: Regirock Radar Regice Radar Registeel Radar

Latias &amp; Latios

The Eon Duo Radar (needs a Soul Dew) locates the Secret Garden. Hoenn Champion Rocco drops both dews: Ruby Dew attracts Latias and Sapphire Dew attracts Latios (level 50–75) at the garden's altar.

Key items: Lati Radar Ruby Dew Sapphire Dew

Kyogre

Craft the Ocean Core (Water Gem + Heart of the Sea) and use it within Kyogre's Submerged Temple, located with the Kyogre Radar. Kyogre appears at level 60–70.

Key items: Ocean Core Kyogre Radar

Groudon

Blast-furnace the Geostone blocks found on Groudon's volcanic island into Molten Crust, craft the Earth Core (Molten Crust + iron block), and summon Groudon (level 60–70) atop the volcano. The Groudon Radar marks the island.

Key items: Earth Core Molten Crust Groudon Radar

Mega Rayquaza

Forge the Sky Core from four treasures: the Aether Gem (from Lyris), Desolate Gem (Magma Leader Max), Primordial Gem (Aqua Leader Ivan) and the Emerald Emblem found in the Sky Pillar itself. Summon Mega Rayquaza (level 70–80) at the top of the Sky Pillar, located with the Mega Rayquaza Radar.

Key items: Sky Core Aether Gem Desolate Gem Primordial Gem Emerald Emblem Rayquaza Radar

Jirachi

Find the Melodic Tape (Vol.1) inside the Wish Cave and play it at Jirachi's shrine to awaken it at level 75–80.

Key items: Melodic Tape Vol 1

Deoxys

A Meteorite Crystal lies at Deoxys' fallen meteorite — use it to call Deoxys back to its shrine at level 75–80. The Deoxys Meteorite block (from Mega Showdown) later lets it change forms.

Key items: Meteorite Crystal

Uxie, Mesprit &amp; Azelf

Defeat the three Team Galactic Commanders to reveal the hidden lakes: Jupiter in the Eterna Building (Acuity Proof), Mars at the Wind Plant (Verity Proof) and Saturn in the Galactic HQ (Valor Proof). Uxie (Lake Acuity), Mesprit (Lake Verity) and Azelf (Lake Valor) await at level 40–65, and each guardian grants its jewel of gratitude — needed to reforge a broken Red Chain.

Key items: Acuity Proof Verity Proof Valor Proof

Dialga &amp; Palkia

Seize the Red Chain by defeating Cyrus at the Galactic HQ — or reforge a Broken Red Chain with the Knowledge, Emotion and Willpower Jewels of the Lake Guardians. Use it on the altars at Spear Pillar (located with the Space-Time Radar) to summon Dialga and Palkia at level 70.

Key items: Red Chain Broken Red Chain Knowledge Jewel Emotion Jewel Willpower Jewel Space Time Radar

Heatran

Combine the Heat Fragment (from Buck) and Heat Splinter (from Galactic scientist Charon) into a Magmatic Cluster, then reheat it in the Stark Forge to restore the Magma Stone. It summons Heatran (level 50–65) at its altar in Stark Mountain, a Nether fortress located with the Heatran Radar.

Key items: Magma Stone Heat Fragment Heat Splinter Heatran Radar

Regigigas

Forge the Titan Relic from the five elemental relics (Cryo, Draco, Metal, Pebble and Spark, mined from their matching LumyMon ores) and use it to awaken Regigigas (level 70–80) inside the Snowpoint Temple, located with the Regigigas Radar.

Key items: Titan Relic Regigigas Radar

Cresselia

The Member Card (from Elfio) reveals Fullmoon Island, Cresselia's dwelling. Weave a Dream Catcher from Dream String (looted in the island's dream side) and a Lunar Wing, then channel it at Cresselia's altar (level 40–65).

Key items: Member Card Dream Catcher Dream String Lunar Wing

Darkrai

Sleep holding the Moonlight Amulet to cross into the Nightmare Dimension on Newmoon Island. Loot Nightmare String there, craft the Nightmare Weaver (amulet + string) and use it at Darkrai's shrine to manifest it (level 40–65). Keep a Lunar Wing handy — it is your only way back out of the shadows.

Key items: Moonlight Amulet Nightmare Weaver Nightmare String Lunar Wing

Manaphy &amp; Phione

The rare Manaphy Egg is recovered from Manaphy's hidden ocean shrine, and Manaphy itself appears there at level 50. Per the pack's advancements, Phione is obtained by hatching an egg (Cobbreeding handles Pokémon breeding and egg hatching in this pack).

Shaymin

Professor Oak's Letter, infused with Gracidea scent, invites Shaymin (level 50–55) to the Flower Paradise shrine — the letter is tied to the "Pallet Town Legacy" advancement (assembling the five first companions of Kanto). Each use fades the letter; restore a Faded Letter with a Gracidea Flower to call Shaymin again.

Key items: Prof Oak Letter Faded Letter

Giratina

Restore the Old Journal from a Torn Journal and Torn Page (both looted in the Team Galactic HQ and Wind Plant) to reveal Turnback Cave. Its depths hold a portal room into the Distortion World — a whole separate dimension — and cave chests can hold the Griseous Key. In the Distortion World, mine Distortion Origin Ore for Raw Origin (smelts to Origin Ingots, and 8 Raw Origin + an iron ingot craft a reusable Distortion Portal). The Giratina Radar then locates Giratina's Island, where the Renegade Pokémon lurks at level 75–95.

Key items: Old Journal Torn Journal Torn Page Griseous Key Distortion Portal Giratina Radar Origin Ingot

Arceus

Craft the Azure Flute from the Temporal Flute (from Damon), the Spatial Flute (from Perula) and an Origin Ingot. Hold right-click to play it and cross the dimensional veil to the Temple of Sinnoh, where Arceus awaits at level 70.

Key items: Azure Flute Temporal Flute Spatial Flute Origin Ingot

Victini

Spawns naturally in the Nether's crimson forest at level 50 (ultra-rare).

Swords of Justice

All four spawn naturally: Cobalion in windswept forests, Terrakion in savannas, Virizion in forests (each level 50–70), and Keldeo at level 60 (rarely 80) in Terralith's sakura groves and valleys.

Forces of Nature

Natural roamers: Tornadus over oceans, Thundurus in mountains, Landorus in badlands during clear weather (all level 50–60), and Enamorus in mountains at level 50.

Tao Trio

Reshiram spawns in mountains during clear weather and Zekrom in mountains during thunderstorms (both level 70–84); Kyurem appears in ice spikes while it rains (level 66–78). Craft the DNA Splicers to fuse Kyurem with Reshiram or Zekrom.

Key items: Dna Splicer

Meloetta

Spawns naturally in sunflower plains at level 50–55.

Genesect

Spawns naturally in dripstone caves at level 48–68.

Xerneas, Yveltal &amp; Zygarde

Xerneas appears in floral biomes by day and Yveltal in spooky biomes at night (both level 65–75, very rare). Zygarde spawns at level 1–10 in dripstone and lush caves — Mega Showdown's Zygarde Cores let it assemble its forms.

Kalos Mythicals

Diancie spawns in lush caves (level 45–68), Hoopa in the End (level 65–75) and Volcanion in mountains (level 70).

Type: Null &amp; Silvally

Team Rocket Scientists drop the Fossilized Helmet — revive it in the Resurrection Machine to create Type: Null. It evolves into Silvally at 160 friendship.

Key items: Fossilized Helmet

Island Guardians (Tapus)

All four Tapus spawn naturally at level 50–70 under open sky: Tapu Koko in savannas and jungles, Tapu Lele in floral biomes and beaches, Tapu Bulu along rivers and jungles, and Tapu Fini along rivers and beaches.

Cosmog line (Solgaleo &amp; Lunala)

Cosmog spawns in the End (level 28–42) and evolves into Cosmoem at level 43, then into Solgaleo (level 53, daytime) or Lunala (level 53, night). Both also spawn directly — Solgaleo in sunny savannas and the End, Lunala in spooky biomes at night and the End.

Key items: N Solarizer N Lunarizer

Necrozma

Necrozma spawns in the End at level 70–75. Its fused forms wait in dedicated End towers: the Necrozma Dusk Radar (built with an N Solarizer) locates the Dusk Tower for Dusk Mane, and the Necrozma Dawn Radar (with an N Lunarizer) the Dawn Tower for Dawn Wings.

Key items: Necrozma Dusk Radar Necrozma Dawn Radar N Solarizer N Lunarizer

Ultra Beasts

Ultra Beasts spawn naturally, mostly in the End and extreme biomes: Nihilego in oceans, Buzzwole in jungles, Pheromosa in Terralith steppes and the End, Xurkitree, Celesteela and Guzzlord in the End, Kartana in cherry groves, Stakataka in the deep dark, Blacephalon in spooky biomes, and Poipole at the End's Dawn and Dusk Towers.

Alolan Mythicals

Magearna spawns in taigas (level 50–85), Marshadow in the deep dark (level 45–68) and Zeraora in badlands and forests (level 45–85).

Meltan &amp; Melmetal

Meltan spawns near placed iron blocks anywhere in the overworld (level 38–48) and on beaches. It evolves into Melmetal at level 70 — while holding an anvil.

Zacian &amp; Zamazenta

Both heroes spawn in dark forests at level 65–80. The Rusted Sword (Luna Henge ruins, Zacian raid bosses) and Rusted Shield (Sol Henge ruins, Zamazenta raid bosses) unlock their Crowned forms.

Key items: Rusted Sword Rusted Shield

Eternatus

Eternatus slumbers in the Eternatus Cocoon, a Legendary Monuments structure generating in the End highlands and midlands. Mine and smelt Galar Particles from Galar Particle Ore, press nine into a Galar Particle Block, and craft the Eternatus Radar to track the cocoon. Eternatus emerges at level 70–90.

Key items: Eternatus Radar Galar Particle Galar Particle Block

Kubfu &amp; Urshifu

Kubfu spawns in cherry groves (level 30–50). Use a Scroll of Darkness for Single Strike Urshifu or a Scroll of Waters for Rapid Strike. Urshifu itself also spawns in cherry groves at level 60–80.

Key items: Scroll Of Darkness Scroll Of Waters

Zarude

Spawns naturally in jungles at level 70.

Regieleki &amp; Regidrago

Both new golems share the Split-Decision Temple (level 70–80), located with the Split-Decision Radar — like the Crown Tundra original, the temple binds Regieleki and Regidrago to a single choice.

Key items: Split Decision Radar

Calyrex, Glastrier &amp; Spectrier

The Calyrex Ice Rider Radar locates the Crown Spire, home of Glastrier (level 60), while the Shadow Rider Radar finds the Crown Cemetery and Spectrier. Both radars require the Reins of Unity. A Calyrex Crown found within these sites, placed upon the statue, summons Calyrex itself (level 50–70) — the Reins then fuse it with its steed.

Key items: Calyrex Ice Radar Calyrex Shadow Radar Calyrex Crown Reins Of Unity

Treasures of Ruin

Four cursed shrines from Legendary Monuments hold the Treasures of Ruin. Their seals drop from Nether loot (bastion, nether fortress and ruined portal chests); combine each seal with a Dark Tera Shard, the matching type gem and a Poké Radar to craft its shrine radar: Wo-Chien Radar → Grasswither Shrine, Chien-Pao Radar → Icerend Shrine, Ting-Lu Radar → Groundblight Shrine and Chi-Yu Radar → Firescourge Shrine. Wo-Chien, Chien-Pao, Ting-Lu and Chi-Yu appear at their shrines at levels 65–80.

Key items: Grasswither Seal Icerend Seal Groundblight Seal Firescourge Seal Wochien Radar Chienpao Radar Tinglu Radar Chiyu Radar

Koraidon &amp; Miraidon

Both box legends spawn naturally on beaches under open sky at level 40.

Loyal Three &amp; Ogerpon

Okidogi roams savannas, Munkidori jungles and Fezandipiti mountain peaks (all level 70–75); Ogerpon spawns in lush biomes at level 50–75.

Terapagos &amp; Pecharunt

Terapagos spawns in deep caves below Y=0 on amethyst blocks (level 55–75). Pecharunt appears in spooky biomes at level 60–80.

Paradox Pokémon

All Paradox Pokémon are natural spawns rather than summons — e.g. Great Tusk in arid biomes, Flutter Mane in spooky biomes and the deep dark, Roaring Moon in mountains at night, Iron Treads in arid mountains, Iron Valiant in plains during thunderstorms, Walking Wake in rainy mountains and Gouging Fire in basalt deltas. Check each species' Pokédex entry for exact conditions.
